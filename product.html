        How to Burn

        01

        Trim the wick to 5mm before every burn. A short wick gives a clean, steady flame and less soot.

        02

        On the first burn, let the wax melt fully to the edges. This prevents tunnelling every time after.

        03

        Never burn more than 3–4 hours at a time. Let the candle cool completely before relighting.

        04

        Keep away from drafts, vents, and anything flammable. Always burn on a heat-resistant surface.
